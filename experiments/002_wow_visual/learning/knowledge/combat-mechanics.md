# Combat mechanics

Class-agnostic facts a fight planner needs, for WoW Forever as a Classic-era client. Each fact is marked:
[live] was observed on this character; [source] is a source claim that has not been checked here. A live
tooltip or observation replaces a source claim. The references came from a web search on 25 Sept 2026.

## Planner facts

- **Global cooldown:** most spells, including instant ones, start a 1.5 s global cooldown before the next spell. [source: Vanilla WoW Wiki, Casting Speed]
- **Pushback:** each melee hit taken during a cast delays it by about 0.5-1 s; a bolt cast in melee often takes 4 s or breaks, and its mana is wasted. [live, 23 Sept]
- **Instant spells** (shocks, weapon imbues) cannot be pushed back. The shocks share one cooldown. [source: Forever tooltips; live tooltip 24 Sept: Earth Shock 6 s cooldown]
- **Range:** each spell's hotkey digit on the action bar turns red while the target is beyond that spell's range, so the bolt and shock digits give distance bands: 30 yd for Lightning Bolt, 20 yd for Earth Shock, about 5 yd for melee. No nameplate means beyond about 40 yd. [live, 23-24 Sept; owner]
- **Facing:** a spell needs the target in front of the character; the game says "Target needs to be in front of you" otherwise. Interact With Target turns the character to face it, even behind. [live, 24 Sept]
- **Speed:** a melee creature runs as fast as the character, so walking away from it only gives it free hits. [live, 23 Sept]
- **Mana:** spending mana stops spirit regeneration for 5 s; drinking restores mana regardless, but only while seated out of combat, and damage interrupts it. [source: Warcraft Wiki, Five second rule; Drink]
- **Misses:** spells miss about 4% of the time against a same-level target, 5-6% against one or two levels higher, 17% against three higher. A bolt that does almost no damage was probably a miss or a resist. [source: Classic WoW Wiki, Spell Hit Chance; live, 23 Sept: one bolt did about 1%]
- **Fleeing:** many humanoids flee at about 15-20% health, towards other creatures; beasts and elementals usually fight on. [source: Vanilla WoW Wiki, Humanoid]
- **Leash:** a creature pulled too far from its spawn evades: it returns home at full health. [source: Warcraft Wiki, Leash]
- **Combat state:** the red ring around the character's portrait shows combat; it lingers a few seconds after a kill. Health regenerates only out of combat. [live, 23 Sept; source: Warcraft Wiki, Regeneration]
