## Class-specific (Shaman / Skyborne)

### Skyborne racials and zone (levels 1–4)

- **Coming of Age** is among the first quests; it rewards an **ancient hair loom** (heirloom-style item) after speaking to an NPC in the starting hub. (`4UiOoQ7uKpI` @ `00:02:05`)
- **Windborne (movement racial):** **+10% mounted and on-foot movement speed** for **30 seconds** if no **elemental convergence** is nearby; **15 minutes** if a convergence is found and used. (`4UiOoQ7uKpI` @ `00:02:25`–`00:02:31`; echoed `WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:12:10`)
- **Elemental Insight:** **+5% damage vs elementals**; **Wind Blast** adds **+1% spell and melee range** (auto-caption wording). (`4UiOoQ7uKpI` @ `00:03:16`–`00:03:25`)
- Starting quests mix **kill**, **gather** (e.g. **15 windstone clusters**), and **use Windborne near elemental convergence** objectives. (`4UiOoQ7uKpI` @ `00:04:12`–`00:05:02`)
- **Enhancement** levelling: streamer notes **melee auto-attacks** simplify **mana recovery** while levelling. (`WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:12:44`–`00:12:53`)

### Trainer unlock order (levels 2–8)

- After **level 2**, pick up **Rockbiter Weapon** from the **shaman trainer**; Rockbiter is a **1-hour** weapon imbue that adds **threat on melee** (useful if tanking or controlling aggro). (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `00:38:54`–`00:39:31`, `00:44:26`; `_8biJBbaZNQ` @ `00:20:00`–`00:20:02`; `lFeEAUvK9fY` @ `04:27:34`)
- **Earthbind Totem** available at **level 6** (trainer text read on stream). (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `01:37:49`–`01:37:52`)
- **Healing Wave (rank 2)** trained alongside early totems. (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `01:38:00`–`01:38:01`)
- At **level 8**: train **Lightning Bolt** and **Lightning Shield** (shield described as **10-minute** duration in trainer readout). (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `01:52:33`–`01:52:36`; `WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:41:17`, `01:55:42` area for trainer visit before 8)
- **Earth Shock** appears around **levels 4–5** on the Orc starter (`_8biJBbaZNQ` @ `00:45:06`–`00:49:08`); expect a similar level band on Skyborne (unverified on Skyborne id).

### Combat and ability usage

- Apply **Rockbiter Weapon** when leaving town; re-apply before the hour expires. (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `00:44:26`)
- Early pull pattern on Orc: **Earth Shock** for damage (~**18** damage cited at one gear point). (`_8biJBbaZNQ` @ `00:49:07`–`00:49:09`)
- **Totem bar** is optional UI; one player disables it and manages totems from the spellbook/action bar. (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `01:42:54`–`01:42:59`)
- **Quest chain for totems:** **Earth Septa** (quest item) gating **first totem** unlock is called out during levelling. (`WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:35:20`–`00:39:49`)
- **Rank management (levels 8+ but relevant early):** keep **multiple ranks** of **Healing Wave** and **Earth Shock** on the bar—low rank for mana efficiency, high rank when needed; avoids overheal. (`bm4xGcBj-DQ` @ `01:28:02`–`01:28:29`)
- **Totem refresh behaviour:** refreshing a totem may **despawn existing totems**—reposition before re-casting in combat. (`bm4xGcBj-DQ` @ `03:31:20`–`03:31:21`) (stream segment above level 12; mechanic likely global)

## Suggested level 1–12 loop (synthesised from videos)

1. Character create → enable **auto loot**, **interact key**, damage meter/CDs if desired (`_8biJBbaZNQ`, `WIwyNfxRiFU`).
2. Accept **every** hub quest (`4UiOoQ7uKpI`, `ThVlyymiLfI`).
3. Use **Windborne** near **elemental convergences** when quests require it (`4UiOoQ7uKpI`).
4. Train **Rockbiter** at first trainer visit after level 2; keep imbue active (`ThVlyymiLfI`, `lFeEAUvK9fY`).
5. Complete **Earth Septa** (or equivalent) quest before expecting totems (`WIwyNfxRiFU`).
6. Return to trainer at **6** (Earthbind + heals) and **8** (Lightning Bolt + Lightning Shield) (`ThVlyymiLfI`, `WIwyNfxRiFU`).
7. Pull with **Earth Shock** / bolt as unlocked; weave **auto-attacks** if playing Enhancement (`_8biJBbaZNQ`, `WIwyNfxRiFU`).

## Most useful for an AI agent

1. Hub quest sweep before combat reduces backtracking (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `00:44:39`).
2. Track trainer breakpoints: **2** Rockbiter, **6** Earthbind, **8** Lightning Bolt/Shield (`ThVlyymiLfI`).
3. Maintain **Rockbiter Weapon** buff (60 min); re-apply on expiry (`ThVlyymiLfI` @ `00:44:26`).
4. Use **elemental convergence** objectives to extend Windborne speed to 15 minutes (`4UiOoQ7uKpI` @ `00:02:25`).
5. For Enhancement, prioritise **melee time on target** for mana (`WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:12:51`).
6. Watch for **Earth Septa**-style quest gating before placing totems (`WIwyNfxRiFU` @ `00:35:20`).
7. Map **quest turn-in NPCs** via map markers even if overhead markers disabled (`2S0MlYfmkxA` @ `00:12:09`).
8. Self-cast heal keybind separate from party-frame heals (`2S0MlYfmkxA` @ `00:03:22`).
9. Use **lowest rank** Earth Shock / Healing Wave unless target HP warrants higher (`bm4xGcBj-DQ` @ `01:28:02`).
10. Beta cap **20**—do not plan talents/spells beyond that in automation tests (`_8biJBbaZNQ` @ `00:11:03`).
