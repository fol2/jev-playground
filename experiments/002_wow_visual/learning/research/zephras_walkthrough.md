# Zephras Isle walkthrough (Skyborne, WoW: Forever)

**Scope:** Level 1 through leaving the island for Azeroth, centred on **Windshaper (Horde)** play but with **class-agnostic** steps called out. Zone level band is **1–12** per Blizzard; beta players often reach **~14** in Valanaar before the exit ship (unverified tuning).
**Data vintage:** September 2026 beta / pre-launch datamining; quest IDs and coordinates from Wowhead Forever where noted. Anything not tied to a primary source is marked **(unverified)**.

---

## Zone map and natural route

Zephras Isle is the Skyborne starting zone in Skywall: failing anchor pylons, vanished wind spirits, and conflict between **Windshapers**, **High Order**, and **Al'Aketh** cultists (Blizzard, [Meet the Skyborne](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302071/wow-forever-meet-the-new-skyborne)).

**Geography (class-agnostic):** Hands-on coverage describes a **U-shaped** path **north-west → south → north-east**: **Thendal Village** (hub) → farmlands/ruins → **Shrine of Alar** (lighting darkens) → **Shadowgate Forest** → **Ruins of Ban'aethal** (late-zone mood; sleeping Altarius seen in demo) ([Classic WoW Forever zone tour](https://classicwowforever.com/guides/wow-forever-zephras-isle-skyborne/)).

**Quest hubs (Blizzplanet order, north → south):**

| Hub | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Thendal Village / Thendal Grove** | Levels 1–5 tutorial, Al'Aketh pressure in the grove |
| **Shen'dar Village** | Levels 6–8+, camping/prideclaws, cult breadcrumb south |
| **Falaath Village / Shen'dar Highlands** | Cult infiltration, bandits, stormcallers |
| **Valanaar** | Levels ~8–14, orchard/forest arcs, **exit storyline** |
| **Shadowglen Forest** | Side hub (hermit) per datamine list **(unverified depth)** |

**Efficient travel spine:** Complete **Thendal** → road south to **Shen'dar** (do **not** take “Welcome to Shen'dar Village” chain until other Shen'dar quests done—Blizzplanet) → cult arc toward **Falaath** → **To Valanaar** → finish **Valanaar** side zones (Windfield Orchard, Shadowgale Forest, Ban'aethal) → **defer** Valenia/Talaanis council quests until you have Valanaar XP → finale **Confront Lorthuna** → leave via **The Earthen Ring** ([Blizzplanet quest compendium](https://warcraft.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/world-of-warcraft-forever-zephras-isle-quests), [Wowhead 95349](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95349/the-earthen-ring)).

## Most useful for an AI agent

1. **Hub detection:** Yellow markers cluster at **Rorian (42, 23.4)** early; shift to **Yala (47.2, 21.8)** for Al'Aketh; then **Aonda (45.6, 45.4)** for Shen'dar.
2. **Quest log OCR:** Track objective counters (8/8 vuldren, 7/7 converts, 6/6 winds **destroyed**, 15/15 windstones).
3. **Class gate:** If log shows **Humming Recall Crystal**, complete **Embracing the Elements** before wandering south.
4. **Faction gate:** **Reading the Ley Lines** should be absent on Windshaper; do not wait for it.
5. **Falling With Style:** Position on tower **43.6, 24.0**; trigger **Walk on Air** mid-fall; confirm **1/1** before running to Rorian.
6. **Anchors of Zephras:** Must pick dialogue **“Halaan, please lend me your gift.”** or objective stays 0/1 ([AllThings](https://allthings.how/how-to-complete-the-anchors-of-zephras-in-wow-forever-full-quest/)).
7. **Al'Aketh Thugs:** Route to **38.3, 30.3** when log shows Malduko objective; kill order: clear neophytes/brutes then named.
8. **Cave heuristic:** **Shreikling Den** = carry NPC quest + pool matriarch—avoid pulling entire tunnel.
9. **Exit condition:** Ship quest **The Earthen Ring**—navigate to Valanaar **dock**, not Stormwind automatically ([Wowhead 95349](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95349/the-earthen-ring)).
10. **Defer council quests:** If Talaanis/Valenia available mid-Valanaar, skip until Blizzplanet side content complete to avoid under-level combat.
