# WoW Forever: Shaman (levels 1–30)

Research brief for an AI questing agent. **Class-specific** facts are under *Shaman*; **class-agnostic** Forever mechanics are under *All classes*. Spell numbers below are from the Forever client unless marked Classic Era comparison.

**Beta caveat (September 2026):** The first public beta capped at **level 20**; a **level 30** test phase and **4 November 2026** launch are advertised. Anything not re-verified in a level 30 build is marked **(unverified)**.

**Primary character context:** Horde **Windshaper Skyborne** Shaman, Zephras Isle (levels 1–12), Windshapers faction.

## Talents (levels 10–30): Enhancement vs Elemental

First point at **10**. At **30** you have **21** points. Forever client: **50** talents per tree with **12 new** Shaman talents (e.g. **Lightning Overload**, **Lava Burst**, **Maelstrom Weapon**, **Riptide**). | https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/ ; https://www.zockify.com/forever/shaman/

**Levels 10–20 — `0/11/0`** (beta-documented):

## Rotations and priority (single target vs two targets)

### Enhancement — single target (levels 1–30)

**Prep:** **Rockbiter** (or **Windfury** at 30+), **Lightning Shield** (reapply in bursts to respect 5-second rule). | Warcraft Tavern Classic rotation; wowforeverbuilds pull guide

**Pull / opener:**

1. Optional **Lightning Bolt** only as **pull** (not mid-fight — resets swing / spirit regen). | https://wowforeverbuilds.com/guide/beta-20-enhancement-shaman-leveling
2. **Earthbind Totem** from level 6 for **earthbind kiting** (stay out of melee except swing windows). | https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/shaman-leveling-guide/
3. From 10: **Searing Totem** on durable targets.
4. **Flame Shock** on cooldown for DoT; **Earth Shock** for interrupt or pre-10 nuke.
5. From 20: weave **Frost Shock** for runners; **Shamanistic Focus** makes shocks sustainable.

**Simplified priority (10–29):** Lightning Shield (maintain) → Earthbind (kite) → Flame Shock → melee autos → Earth Shock (interrupt) → Searing Totem.

### Enhancement — two targets (no Chain Lightning before 32)

1. Stack mobs on **Searing Totem** / active **Fire Totem**.
2. **Fire Nova** (12+) when both are within **10 yd of the fire totem** (**10 s** CD).
3. From 26, **Magma Totem** adds **8 yd** pulse — drop instead of or after Searing for stationary packs.
4. **Flame Shock** both only if mana allows after **Shamanistic Focus**; otherwise tab-target melee and one shock finisher.

### Elemental — single target

1. Max range **Lightning Bolt**.
2. **Flame Shock** if full duration will tick.
3. Skip expensive totems on single weak mobs; on longer fights: optional **Stoneskin/Strength of Earth**, **Searing** last (shorter duration). | https://leprestore.com/guides/world-of-warcraft-forever/wow-forever-elemental-shaman-guide-best-builds-race-professions/
4. **Fire Nova** only with an active fire totem and enemies in range.

### Elemental — two targets (levels 12–30)

- Same as ST but prioritize **Fire Nova** when **both** are near the fire totem (still best at **3+** mobs; for exactly two, treat as “mini cleave”).
- Maintain **Flame Shock** on one; **Lightning Bolt** focus target.
- **Magma Totem** (26+) if they stay stacked **20 s**.
- **Chain Lightning** not until **32** — do not plan around it before leaving the 1–30 band.

Source for Elemental cleave notes: Leprestore Elemental rotation; Icy Veins Elemental Forever (Fire Nova requires fire totem).

## Most useful for an AI agent

1. At **level 4**, complete **Call of Earth** at the **Windshaper shaman trainer** before buying **Stoneskin** — you need the **Earth Totem** relic in bags.
2. Track **four totem items** in inventory; without the correct relic, totem spells fail silently or error — verify after each quest turn-in.
3. Default solo build: **Enhancement `0/11/0` by 20**, then **Flurry** — prioritize **Shamanistic Focus** before spamming shocks.
4. Combat loop 6–29: **Earthbind** + **kite** + **swing timer**; only enter melee for auto-attack windows (Warcraft Tavern earthbind shuffle).
5. Use **Lightning Bolt** only as a **pull** once melee build is active; mid-fight casts waste mana and swing time.
6. **Fire Nova** requires an **active fire totem** and enemies within **10 yd of that totem** — agent must position or use **Totemic Projection** (22+).
7. At **10**, fire totem quest (Skyborne: **Olariaan** chain); at **20**, water attunement before **Healing Stream**; at **30**, air attunement before **Windfury Totem** (32) and **Grounding**.
8. From **20**, bind **Call of the Elements** to drop four-bar setup; **Totemic Recall** before mounting/long travel.
9. **Mana:** drink between pulls; batch **Lightning Shield** / **Flame Shock** refreshes; after 20 Enh, shocks are cheap enough for rotation.
10. **UI:** read shock cooldowns (**6 s**), **Fire Nova** (**10 s**), totem durations (**Searing** 30–40 s scaling) — re-place only when farming packs, not every kill.
