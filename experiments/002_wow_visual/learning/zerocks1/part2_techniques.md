# Techniques — Zerocks, Skyborne Shaman, 10m00s–19m59s

Frames are 640×270, so chat, quest-tracker lines, and most nameplates are too soft to read. Notes below use what is large enough to see: windows, unit frames, the cast bar, floating numbers, and the yellow "Level 3" banner. Quest titles are not quoted.

## 1. Camera and looking around

- Third person the whole way; the back of the white-haired character is visible, including a close over-the-shoulder view on the ramps at 13m10s and 15m43s. No first person.
- Field camera sits medium-far and slightly above, horizon high, character small in the centre (10m00s–10m35s, 16m36s–17m05s). Indoors and on walkways they zoom in until the weapon and hair fill the middle (15m35s–16m11s).
- They yaw hard while moving: down the slope at 10m15s, across the tents at 11m56s, up into the canopy at 14m18s, and along the canyon at 18m16s. The horizon tips during the flick at 14m16s.
- Pitch comes up for buildings and flying mobs: the treehouse roof at 13m32s, the wasp overhead at 17m30s, the tower exterior at 15m57s.
- The minimap stays top-right. They still open the full island map whenever the route is unclear (10m35s, 12m42s, 13m43s, 13m59s, 14m04s, 14m20s, 15m31s, 17m44s).

## 2. Target choice and distance

- 10m04s the first pull is a beast already beside them on the slope, a few yards off, with a white melee arc in the same second the red circle appears.
- They retarget at 10m07s ("Changed Target!") onto the beast that is actually hitting them ("Attacking You!" at 10m08s). A second beast stands in the same cluster and is not ignored.
- Through 13m54s–17m20s they run past fields of red corpses, live beasts other people are hitting, and loose wasps. Dead and contested mobs are skipped.
- 17m27s–17m33s they single out one oversized wasp, walk under it, and only then put the red circle down. Hits that follow are 8–10, and at 17m41s they turn away while it is still flying.
- From 18m30s pulls are again at a few yards, one mob, on the road they are already walking. A yellow cast bar shows at 19m01s with the mob already in frame, then a blue-white impact (19m02s).
- Friendly players get a click and a menu (10m00s, whisper row highlighted) and are then dropped. Green-named village NPCs are run past until a "?" is the one they want (18m20s).

## 3. Fight rhythm

- Beast scuffle 10m04s–10m17s, then another 10m21s–about 10m34s. Floating hits are about 5–6. A purple burst shows at 10m11s and 10m27s. They orbit the camera while still swinging (10m27s–10m29s).
- Road kills from 18m30s last about 8–12 seconds: 18m30s–18m40s, then 18m43s–18m56s, then 19m00s–19m07s, then 19m09s–19m14s, then 19m50s–19m57s. White numbers land around 8–21.
- The main bar's left side is a red-ringed icon, a blue lightning icon, and a green icon (10m04s). At 19m54s the leftmost blue icon has a yellow pressed border while "Level 3" is up. The cast is the short yellow bar above the action bar (19m01s).
- Melee and the spell overlap in the same second (white swing plus blue impact at 19m02s), with the mob already in swing range.
- Health stays a full green bar in every fight here. A green floating 41 at 18m52s may be a heal (unsure). No food or drink icon is used between these kills.
- They chain the next pull as soon as the target frame drops (18m40s to 18m43s, 19m07s to 19m09s). Purple XP numbers (+16 to +45) pop on the kill.

## 4. Extra enemies

- The second beast at 10m06s–10m11s produces "Losing Threat" then "High Threat" (10m24s–10m26s). They swap target and keep hitting; they do not run.
- Other players' spells (purple and blue) are everywhere in the corpse fields (16m37s, 16m47s, 17m52s). They do not assist.
- The wasp grove is a swarm (17m06s–17m29s). They look at several, commit to the big one, and leave when the numbers stay small.
- At the standing stones several people are already fighting (19m24s–19m32s). They take one mob and walk out at 19m33s.
- A red banner across the top at 17m18s (group-invite style, wording unreadable) is gone by 17m19s. They keep running alone.

## 5. Recovery

- No sit, eat, or drink from 10m00s to 19m59s. The next pull always starts with the green bar still full (18m30s, 19m00s, 19m50s).
- Mana stays a visible blue bar, including after the casts at 19m01s–19m11s. They do not stop to drink before the level-up pull.
- After the 10m kills they open the map (10m35s). After the vendor they run straight out.

## 6. Quest flow

- Character window stays open while they run toward the village (10m40s–10m47s), then the spellbook (10m48s–11m01s), including one spell's detail page at 10m56s. They do not stop to read.
- Quest parchment at the blue tents, 11m04s–11m08s, with the cursor on a reward. They close it and move.
- Vendor list at the wooden cart, 11m15s–11m50s. Tooltips and a comparison pane; the list goes blank for a few seconds at 11m33s (tab change, unsure). They stay on the tooltips across that whole half-minute.
- Quest window again on that building at 11m53s, then they leave by 11m55s.
- Up the ramp into the hall, 12m00s–12m11s: talk to the marked NPC, read, window changes, then they turn and leave. Chat shows a completion-style line (wording soft).
- Another parchment on the road at 12m21s–12m26s, reward tooltip, then the quest log at 12m27s–12m31s.
- Map at 12m42s–12m49s shows two yellow quest blobs, zoomed out to the whole island at 12m45s, then back. Quest log again at 12m51s–12m54s before they pick a gate.
- Bridge quest at 13m16s–13m23s, then the treehouse interior at 13m36s–13m39s. A black notice bar sits at the top of those frames; they still finish the dialog and walk out at 13m40s.
- They climb the tower (15m35s–16m05s), look around the top room with the central pedestal, and leave without a quest window.
- Turn-in on the canyon path the moment a "?" is in view: 18m20s approach, 18m22s parchment, 18m23s reward page, running again at 18m24s.
- "Level 3" in yellow at 19m54s–19m59s, during the last kill. They keep swinging through it (white 14 and 15, purple XP), spellbook still closed.

## 7. Route and navigation

- Off the slope they follow the cobbles into the tent village (10m36s–11m12s).
- Village loop: tents, cart vendor, hall on the ramp, back through the square, stone gate (11m12s–13m05s).
- They run through the round trainer room (13m01s) and stay on the curved ramp.
- Out of town they follow the path, then cut the corpse field toward a hill camp (13m54s–14m41s), using the fallen log as a line (14m12s–14m17s).
- Second vendor stop, then a specific small house (15m18s–15m24s), then the big hall. Map is opened at the foot of the ramp before they commit (15m31s).
- Inside, they take the spiral up and the outside walkway (15m39s–15m58s). The ground-floor NPCs stay behind them.
- Back down, interface menu, then the pine field and the canyon road (16m25s–18m20s). After the turn-in they stay on that road into the standing stones (18m24s–19m59s).
- Corpse-filled ground is treated as "already done": they cross it (14m00s, 16m42s, 18m03s) and only start killing once the road is clear of other people's bodies (18m30s).

## 8. UI and keys

- Bottom bar with gryphon ends, seen at 10m04s and 19m54s. Left slots: red-ringed icon, blue lightning icon, green icon, then a cyan orb and a blue square; several dim slots; a yellow icon and a red icon toward the right. Bags are a separate row at the lower right the whole time.
- The pressed slot gets a yellow border (19m54s). No hotkey turns an obvious out-of-range red in this stretch; mobs are fought up close.
- Windows used, in order: unit menu (10m00s), world map (10m35s), character (10m40s), spellbook (10m48s), quest parchment (11m04s), vendor (11m15s and 14m48s), quest log (12m27s, 12m51s), Esc menu then the action-bar options page (16m18s–16m23s).
- Player frame is the lower centre-left portrait (white hair, green bar, blue bar). Target frame sits to its right and goes red in combat. Quest tracker stays under the minimap even while they open the log to actually read.
- Floating combat text is on: threat phrases, white damage, purple XP, yellow "Level 3".

## Surprises

- They read the character sheet and the spellbook while the ground is still scrolling, and they stand in a vendor for half a minute comparing tooltips.
- They open the world map eight times in ten minutes, including once with a live beast beside them (14m08s) and once while leaving the wasp (17m44s). The tracker alone does not pick the route.
- They right-click a player, rest the cursor on whisper, and close the menu without sending anything (10m00s–10m01s).
- They walk to the top of the tower, look at the pedestal room, and walk back down with no quest window (15m59s–16m11s).
- Mid-travel they open Esc and the action-bar settings (16m18s–16m24s), then carry on.
- They try the big wasp, watch 8–10s tick, and drop it while it is still in the air (17m33s–17m41s).
- They decline or clear a group banner without breaking stride (17m18s) and never join the public fights in the corpse fields.
- After 18m30s they chain five short kills at full health with no food. Loot is a glance (18m58s, 19m15s). They are still casting when "Level 3" appears.
