# WoW Forever Shaman Levelling Techniques

Extracted from Tiqqle's WoW Forever streams covering Shaman level 1-20.

## 1. Spell Rotation, Opener & Finisher

- Use **Searing Totem** as primary damage source at low level (clocks ~15 damage early on). (IWhilIN2R1A 0:10:31)
- **Fire Nova** is your primary AoE: 6 second cooldown, comparable to Paladin Consecration in TBC. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:44:02)
- **Chain Lightning** got nerfed in Forever but can overload; 2-3 second cast time; use as AoE filler after Fire Nova. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:39:25, 0:40:59)
- **Earth Shock** generates significant threat and acts as a pseudo-taunt for threat recovery. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:48:42, 1:51:45)
- **Lightning Bolt** is faster cast (2.5 base) compared to Chain Lightning (3 second base). (IWhilIN2R1A 4:13:52, 4:13:20)
- Flame Shock via weapon enchant (**Flametongue** on one-hander) deals bonus damage; experiment with one-hander + spell power vs two-hander. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:42:00, gETjq4-k4qU 0:08:59)
- **Rockbiter** weapon enchant generates high threat for tanking; theory that it scales with damage and spell power. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:29:42, 1:54:30)
- **Lightning Shield** threat generation: consider keeping it up for tank setup; unclear if it generates passive threat. (IWhilIN2R1A 1:30:01, 1:58:11)
- **Storm Strike** adds buff for enhanced AoE output; works with windfury procs for massive damage. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:23:02, 0:39:25)
- Shocking (Earth Shock / chain shocking) should be prioritized to maintain threat. (IWhilIN2R1A 3:09:19)
- Fire Nova requires fire totem to be active; spell version consumes two globals (totem + spell cast). (IWhilIN2R1A 4:03:10, 3:52:46)
- Improved Fire Nova is very strong talent pick. (IWhilIN2R1A 2:58:14)
- Don't sit down between mobs—mana sustain is so good you won't need to rest between kills. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:45:36, 0:88:00)

## 2. Pulling & Target Selection

- Pull one mob at a time when possible; avoid elite packs entirely at low level. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:08:29)
- Be aware mob pulling range; mobs will patrol and add if you're not careful with placement. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:10:00)
- **Totems pull aggro** (confirmed by testing and tank runs); account for this when placing fire totems. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:11:33, 0:13:09, 0:31:11)
- Searing Totem is still functional for threat but watch if patrol packs aggro on it. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:13:39)
- Line-of-sight pulling not mentioned; standard melee range pulls. (No specific LoS example found)
- Neutral vs hostile mobs: you can tag quests humanoids early without consequence. (IWhilIN2R1A multiple quest locations)

## 3. Mana & Health Management

- You almost never sit down between mobs at low level—mana sustain is "pretty smooth." (IWhilIN2R1A 0:45:36)
- Always carry food (cook bore meat early) and water; sitting is rare but possible if AOE-heavy. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:25:36)
- **Strength potions** stack with healing potions and minor force potions for big pulls. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:26:06)
- Healing potions are learnt from First Aid trainer, not potions master. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:26:06)
- Drink to full mana before big dungeon pulls; you can drink while mobs approach if safe. (IWhilIN2R1A 3:28:11)
- Mana costs vary by rank: Rank 1 spells use less mana but do less damage. (IWhilIN2R1A 4:18:59)
- Fire Nova uses a lot of mana; mana becomes the limiting factor for AoE spam. (IWhilIN2R1A 3:48:29, 3:49:00)
- Health threshold unclear but you can pull additional mobs if you're confident in healing output.
- Don't bring extra food if you're good at managing damage (one streamer regretted dropping food unnecessarily). (IWhilIN2R1A 0:41:30)

## 4. When Things Go Wrong (Adds, Fleeing, Death)

- If a mob adds, continue attacking and use Fire Nova on the pack; don't panic kite. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:10:00)
- Kiting is possible but not ideal at low level; focus on threat and AoE instead. (IWhilIN2R1A 3:42:38)
- Earth Shock did not taunt in one case (unclear mechanic); it generates threat but may not pull aggro reliably. (IWhilIN2R1A 1:51:45)
- Corpse runs are standard; always have your path to the dungeon entrance memorized. (IWhilIN2R1A various dungeon runs)
- "I am in danger" moment when over-geared and pulled hard mobs—safe to run and re-engage. (IWhilIN2R1A 0:37:51)
- Paladin and other party members can steal threat; coordinate who pulls what. (IWhilIN2R1A 3:27:40)
