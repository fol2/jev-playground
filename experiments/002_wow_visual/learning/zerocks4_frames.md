## Top 8 Techniques an AI Shaman Should Copy

1. **Pre-pull totem placement** (t=20-40s, t=380-400s, t=700-730s): Drop Searing Totem 2–3 seconds before engaging mob in melee; totem does 30–40% of mob's damage while player handles other tasks.

2. **Lightning Bolt pull from 30+ yards** (t=15-25s, t=370-385s, t=720-740s): Cast from distance to initiate combat safely; mob travels to player while player prepares cooldowns; never static while mob approaches.

3. **Immediate melee after opener** (t=40-60s, t=410-430s, t=750-770s): After Lightning Bolt and totem are deployed, close to melee range (5-8 yards) and attack with weapon; melee + totem + castable heals create redundant damage while minimizing downtime.

4. **Healing Wave when <50% health** (t=100-120s, t=600-630s, t=1150-1180s): Cast Healing Wave immediately when player health drops below 50%; don't wait for critical; interrupt if needed (though not seen here) to deal with mob damage spike.

5. **Sit for 20–40 seconds every 2–3 kills** (t=150-190s, t=500-550s, t=850-920s, t=1250-1320s): Recover mana and health together; never pull next mob while mana is below 70% or health below 70%; prioritizes sustainability over speed.

6. **Avoid multi-mob pulls unless necessary** (t=600-700s, occasionally, but rare): Single-mob pulls are faster overall; if 2 mobs are pulled, use Chain Lightning or Frost Shock to manage damage; kite if health drops below 40%.

7. **Check patrol paths and mob spacing before engaging** (t=100-200s, t=500-550s): Brief pauses before pulls suggest checking nameplate positions; wait for patrols to pass; never pull a mob that has a patrol nearby.

8. **Route between quest hubs efficiently** (t=200-300s, t=700-800s, t=1300-1400s): Follow roads when visible, then cut cross-country toward quest objective markers on minimap; don't backtrack; maintain momentum between quest objectives and combat.
