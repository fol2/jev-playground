## Material findings

### P1 — a structurally broken corpus could pass [fixed in this patch]

`video_jev.py` previously counted valid rows instead of rejecting invalid ones.
`tools/motor_offline.py` accepted totals from 230 upward. Dropping or invalidating
one of 236 rows could therefore retain a green result. The fact minimum used an
`assert`, which disappears under `python -O`.

The replacement validates all rows and exactly the registered 93/75/68 files,
rejects ambiguous option/JSON formats, and uses explicit exceptions. It runs its
negative regressions as part of `--check`. No unrelated routing exemption was added.
Actual corpus compatibility still needs the exact-head `--check`; the local fixture
suite is not a substitute for that run.

### P1 — disappearing tracker text can become false completion [runtime open]

In `../m4/Hunt.swift`, `remaining(_:in:)` treats an absent objective as completed.
`runHunt` returns `OBJECTIVES_COMPLETE` when this result is empty. In
`../m4/HuntProbe.swift`, `survey()` rejects empty OCR text, but accepts a non-empty
text list even when `parseTracker` loses an objective or parses no counts.
Consequently a title-only or partially unreadable tracker can satisfy the completion
path without an observed completion. This is a source-level failure scenario, not
an observed live incident in this review.

Required correction: preserve missing tracked objectives as unknown. Accept explicit
readable `done >= need` or a separately verified turn-in transition as completion,
not disappearance alone. Required regressions: a lost row among several objectives,
title-only OCR, collapsed tracker, objective reappearance, and genuine completion.
Do not merely add more identical reads: repeated OCR failure is not confirmation.

### P1 — no frame becomes apparently healthy/out of combat [runtime open]

`LiveHuntHost.vitals()` returns `HuntObs()` when `feed.latest` is missing; its defaults
are full health, full mana and not in combat. This substitutes an optimistic state
for missing evidence in action polling. This review does not establish how often
that boundary occurs, or certify the entire frame-feed freshness implementation.

Required correction: carry validity, frame identity and age; stop/release input on
missing or over-age vital observations according to the motor contract. Regression
proof should inject absent/frozen frames during walking, turning and recovery.
Also test state changing while a Jev request is pending: a previously admissible
non-combat action must be rechecked before execution. These Swift changes are not
silently bundled into a source-only Python patch without the relevant Mac proof.

### P2 — source uncertainty was erased at the model boundary [fixed, not certified]

The old KB regex removed bracketed trust labels and everything following them,
including source references. The new loader preserves the full bullet and adds its
source location; it does not automatically resolve conflicts or certify provenance.
The schema's fenced YAML remains documentation. A future selector should retrieve
only facts matching build, faction, level, rank and decision context, not send every
claim on every decision. Measure the token/latency trade-off rather than assume it.

The old numeric baseline remains explicitly labelled historical. In particular,
`knowledge/conflicts.md` leaves the regeneration model unresolved; retaining a
baseline for comparison must not promote its five-second rule to verified truth.

### P2 — reported replay results were not reconstructible [documented, outputs improved]

The immutable learning tree has no `zerocks1/runs/`. `regen_probe.jsonl` has five
questions but no paired predictions. Therefore 153/236, the +2 KB difference, the
4/5 selected-probe change and the 30-40% mismatch estimate remain reported results.
Do not backfill made-up predictions. The evaluator now records every prediction,
full probabilities and evaluator/fact/input hashes for future authorised runs.

The README's suggested lower-bound interpretation was removed. One demonstrated
human action is not the only acceptable action; agreement is not safety or task
success. A selected-misses intervention and adjacent frames from one session are
not independent held-out evidence.

## Annotation issues to address before more prompt tuning

`zerocks1/part3_decisions.jsonl` at `20m43s` describes the target as having a bar
"about to reset". Treat this as a hindsight-leakage candidate to relabel from a
strict pre-action crop, not as a proven visual annotation error. Post-action frames
may establish the action/outcome, but cannot supply facts unavailable at decision time.

Separate player level from target level, and use nullable objective membership,
activity, aggro ownership, available trained abilities and observation age. Do not
convert unreadable into false, and do not infer objective credit solely because an
actor attacked a similarly named creature. Compare tracker progress before/after
with ambiguous cases retained for review.

The rapid mana/health changes around `20m51s`–`21m04s` in the existing annotations
are not a controlled regeneration measurement. Possible level changes, external
heals, buffs, consumables, crowd effects and reading errors must be ruled out from
actual footage before assigning a universal refill time. No cause was visually
confirmed in this review.
